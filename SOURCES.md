### Auto-captions: what was and wasn't checked

About a quarter of the citations (OOO, WIH, TBS, SOL, SCET) rest on YouTube auto-captions. We
re-fetched them and checked every quote from these sources against that caption text. We have
**not** checked the captions against the audio, so a quote can match the captions exactly and still
contain a transcription error. Where an error is obvious it is marked `[sic: ...]`.

## How claims are labelled

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Quote with a source ID | Nikita's own words, unless the text says otherwise |
| *Lenny says*, *the host* | Said by the interviewer; Nikita confirmed or built on it |
| *Nikita relays...* | Someone else's words, quoted by Nikita |
| *the team said* | TechCrunch credits it to the tbh team, not Bier by name |
| *his claim* / *by his estimate* | Nikita's own unverified number, or one he hedged |
| *Our reading* / *Our suggestion* / *invented* | This repo's synthesis, thresholds or example numbers, not his |
| `...` inside a quote | Words left out between two exact fragments |
| `[sic]`, `[sic: ...]` | An error in the source text (often an auto-caption error), kept as found |
| `[2021]`, `[2024]`, `[2026]` | True as of that date; re-check before relying on it |
| ⚠️ **Conflict** | Sources disagree; all versions are shown |

## Looked at and not used

- **Where It Happens, after 26:47**: Nikita had left the episode.
- **Host statements presented as facts** (e.g. a "100 million dollars in a hundred days" headline on
  Where It Happens, growth figures from the TBS host): not attributed to Nikita.
- **Second-hand summaries** (blogs, Reddit, LinkedIn posts, *lessons from Nikita* videos): left out on
  purpose. Several repeat wrong figures.
- **The Sources interview body**: paywalled, so only its free intro is used.
- **v1 material removed in v2**: most of the X product changelog (bots, spam rules, country of origin,
  creator payouts, chat), a second-hand line about critics, and a 2022 post that quotes an unrelated
  thread. None of it was needed for the founder lessons.
- **The original post behind the *20% fewer invites per year of age* heuristic** read out by Lenny
  [LP 11:50]: not located, so the figure is attributed to the host's reading.
