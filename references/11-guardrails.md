# 11 · Guardrails (always applied)

## Nikita's own rules

> "I always do things by the book when it comes to operating legally within the compliance
> framework." (1:00:35)

He warns founders that growth tricks outside the rules cause "way more trouble down the line"
and will "burn users too." The patterns he names: using user data in the background "in ways
that it shouldn't be used" and inviting people on a user's behalf (1:01:07).

**The internet defends itself.** He compares the internet to the Gaia hypothesis: "if you do the
wrong thing by users, the internet will come back and get even and defend itself." (1:01:21 to 1:01:55)

## Design so nobody is left out

tbh and Gas only allowed positive things, through polls the team wrote (27:42). Gas also showed
users' names in polls more often if they had not been voted on recently, so everyone got a vote:
"we wanted to spread the love in every way possible." (1:03:52)

Nikita recalls Gas receiving a message every day from a user saying they had reconsidered suicide
or self-harm (1:03:14). "We were entirely focused on making teens feel better." (1:03:42)

## This skill's refusals

The skill will not help with:
- Sending invites, texts or messages a user did not knowingly send.
- Using contact or other personal data beyond what the user understood and agreed to.
- Fake social proof, fake users or fake activity.
- Anonymous mechanics that allow bullying or negative messages about real people.
- Any mechanic aimed at minors that relies on pressure, shame or deception.

**For apps used by minors:** remind the user that laws on children's data and messaging apply
and differ by country, and that they need proper legal review. This skill is not legal advice.
