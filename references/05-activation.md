# 05 · Activation: value in three seconds

> "If you can't demonstrate value in the first three seconds, it's over." (1:27:37)

His reasoning: attention is spread across so many notifications and products. For a social
app, "you have to sign up and then the first night you have to see all of your friends on the
app and experience it, otherwise you'll churn." (1:27:46)

## Every tap is a miracle

> "Every tap on a mobile app is a miracle for you as a product developer because users will turn
> and bounce to their next app very quickly." (14:35)

## Invert the time to value: the Dupe case (1:27:58 to 1:29:28)

- Dupe was a shopping app with many features. One feature found the cheapest version of a
  product from its page. Nikita: "That should be a whole company."
- The mechanic: type the domain in front of an existing product URL. Short domain required, so
  the founder bought Dupe.com.
- Videos went viral, users remembered the trick. By Nikita's estimate, "millions in ARR" in
  under 60 days of launching.
- He is explicit that this is not guaranteed: roughly half his advisees hit that level of
  success and half fail outright.

## Use every API, in non-traditional ways

> "Extraordinary product people are deeply aware of every possible API and how it can be used
> in non-traditional ways." (1:30:30)

Example: contact sync can find all your friends AND rank the people not on the app yet by how
many friends they have on it, exposing a lot of value in one tap (1:30:59).

## 10,000 taps versus one

> "A lot of founders will go and say, 'Oh, they can just exchange usernames and that's how they
> can add each other.' That is the most unrealistic thing ever." (1:31:17)

Typing usernames 50 times for a 50-person friend list: "we're looking at 10,000 taps versus one." (1:31:35)

**Checks to run:**
- Time a new user from open to first moment of real value. Over three seconds of effort before value is a finding.
- Count taps to a full friend graph. Username exchange is a red flag.
- Is there a one-line, memorable mechanic (like a URL trick) that teaches the value instantly?
- Does the first session show the user their friends already there?
