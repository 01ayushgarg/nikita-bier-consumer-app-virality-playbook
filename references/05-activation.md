# 05 · Activation: value in three seconds

> "if you can't demonstrate value in the first three seconds, it's over." [LP 1:27:31]

For a social app, "you have to sign up and then the first night you have to see all of your friends
on the app and experience it, otherwise you'll churn." [LP 1:27:43]

## Every tap is a miracle

> "every tap on a mobile app is a miracle for you as a product developer because users will turn and
> bounce to their next app very quickly." [LP 14:29]

## Invert the time to value: the Dupe case [LP 1:27:43 to 1:29:37]

- Dupe was a shopping app with many features. One feature found a cheaper version of a product
  from its page. Nikita: "that should be a whole company." [LP 1:28:23]
- The mechanic: type the domain in front of an existing product URL. That needs a short domain,
  so the founder bought dupe.com [LP 1:28:53].
- Videos spread and users remembered the trick. By Nikita's estimate, “millions in arr” within
  about 60 days of launching [LP 1:29:12]. **His claim.**
- He is explicit that this is not guaranteed: roughly half his advisees hit that level and half fail.

## Use every API, in non-traditional ways

> "extraordinary product people are deeply aware of every possible api and how it can be used in
> non-traditional ways." [LP 1:30:32]

His example: contact sync that finds your friends and also "ranks the people who are not on the app
yet but have a bunch of friends on it" [LP 1:30:59].

## 10,000 taps versus one

Exchanging usernames is "the most unrealistic thing ever": for a 50-person friend list "we're looking
at 10,000 taps versus one." [LP 1:31:27 to 1:31:38]

## Design is the highest-ROI work

"consumer products live and die in the pixels." He calls mapping out every direction the product
could take, and seeing what each would look like, the highest-return work there is [SOL 3:01].

## Where installs actually happen

In 2025 he wrote that most installs happen in bed or on the toilet, so requiring sound or voice in
sign-up means accepting a “60% tax” on growth.
[X 2025-05-21](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1925179335180197902) The 60% figure is his estimate,
not a measured statistic he published.

## Product sense is predicting conversion

His definition, January 2023: you can look at a screen and predict the share of users who reach the
next one "within a 10% margin of error."
[X 2023-01-23](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1617590526353764353) He later quoted that post when
he said he had guessed an app's push opt-in rate almost exactly
[X 2025-05-11](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1921708920181055975).

He used it at X: "i was like that's going to convert at 45%. that's going to convert at 65... i've just
seen so many dashboards on these funnels" [OOO 15:25 to 15:29].

## Fix the obvious first

His 2021 joke: the slow PM studies the funnel for hours for a 5% gain, the smart PM makes “a button
bigger” for 20%. [X 2021-08-13](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1426229686175027201)

## How to apply it (our reading)

**The 10-minute activation walk.**

1. Install the app on a clean phone. Start a stopwatch and a tap counter.
2. Write each screen in order. Next to it, write your predicted conversion before you look at any
   data (his product-sense test).
3. Stop the clock at the first moment of real value: friends shown, a result returned, a message
   received.
4. Compare predictions with the funnel. The biggest gap between predicted and actual is where to
   look first, and often the fix is the “button bigger” kind.
5. Remove or defer every screen before value that does not raise the chance of value (intro
   slides, profile photo, long forms).

**Worked numbers (invented).** Seven screens before value, each converting 85%: 0.85^7 is about
32% of installs reaching value. Cutting two screens (0.85^5) gives about 44%, a 37% relative lift
with no new feature. A required voice note converting 40% would cut the original 32% to about 13%.

**Failure modes.**
- Asking for sign-up before showing anything the user wants.
- Contacts permission asked cold, with no visible reason.
- Value that depends on other people arriving later (matching that takes hours).
- Measuring *activation* as account created rather than value received.

**Limits.** The three-second line is a rule of thumb, not a measured threshold. Apps that need
trust first (money, health) may need one explanatory screen; keep it to one.
