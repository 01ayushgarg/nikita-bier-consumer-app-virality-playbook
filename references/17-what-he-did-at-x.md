# 17 · What he did at X, and what founders can take from it

A short, dated record of his product work at X, kept to the parts that teach something about
growing a consumer app. Results are **his claims** unless a third party is cited.

## The dates

- **Before the job:** adviser to xAI on product and growth, for about a year [OOO 12:03]. The hiring
  *interview* was a weekend: "we work basically for like 48 hours straight build a beautiful
  prototype of the onboarding flow" [OOO 14:35].
- **2025-06-30:** announced he was joining X as head of product
  [X 2025-06-30](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1939723101723574703); reported the next day [TC5].
- **2026-08-05:** stepped back from leading product and stayed on as an adviser. "...it's time to
  pass the torch and demote myself to my natural state: a poster."
  [X 2026-08-05](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2085105586966827343), [TC6]
- ⚠️ MediaPost says “the past 15 months” [MP]; the dates above give about 13.

## Five lessons from the work

### 1. Audit every funnel on a mature app, too

His aim was to stop a "trajectory uh toward irrelevance" [OOO 9:10], and the method was the one from
his startups: "we audited every every possible funnel to get into the app." [OOO 9:33]

⚠️ **Results, two versions.** In 2026 he said the app went "from number 78 in the app store to a peak
position of number two" within about the first 30 days [OOO 9:46]. His post two months into the job
says No. 78 at the start and "today we opened the [sic] at No. 6"
[X 2025-09-09](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1965421715732759000). At exit: "We've climbed 70
spots in the App Store since this time last year." [X 2026-08-05]

### 2. On an interest graph, the new-user ramp is the growth lever

"we will have succeeded if when a plumber signs up for x, they can find other plumbers." [TBS 32:15]
His tools were **Starterpacks** (curated accounts per niche) and **Custom Timelines**, launched
2026-04-21, which let users pin one of “over 75 topics” to the home tab
[X 2026-04-21](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2046736181002645520). He calls both “escape valves” for
gaps in the main algorithm [X 2026-04-25](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2047909972990927255). For
Starterpacks he claims new users' time spent doubled [OOO 27:33].

### 3. When reach drops, look for a missing signal

Links got low reach because the in-app browser covered the post, so people forgot to react and the
ranking had no signal [X 2025-10-19](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1979994223224209709). "it was
just a byproduct of the ui." [OOO 40:20] The fix kept the post visible while reading.

### 4. Ship with a guardrail metric

For the link change, link sessions went “way up” while time spent “was actually flat” [OOO 38:35 to
38:37]. For an algorithm migration the bar was whether engagement could “stay at least flat”, and,
he says, "it did and then it it's it's been up higher than any time in history" [OOO 20:45].

### 5. Test your incentives, and expect surprises

He reported a 3% experiment in which removing the 30 highest-paid revenue-share accounts from the
For You timeline raised both time spent and daily active users.
[X 2026-07-01](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2072203879479910490) That is what the post says; it
does not say what caused the result.

## How the team worked

"we come up with an idea. uh we we build it in a week and it's it's out, you know, at facebook things
would take months" [OOO 41:19]. Ideas were previewed in public before launch (chapter 13).

## How to apply it (our reading)

- **Old product, same method.** Run the chapter 08 diagnosis on a mature app before assuming its
  growth problem is the brand.
- **Interest graph?** Give new users a curated start (a few dozen accounts, topics or examples for
  their niche) and measure day-7 time spent for new users as the target.
- **Low reach on a format?** Check whether the UI hides the actions that feed your ranking.
- **Every launch** names one guardrail metric before it ships.
- **Rewards change behaviour.** Before paying users for any activity, run a small holdout and watch
  quality, not only volume.

**Limits.** These are his accounts of his own work at a very large company. Most numbers have no
independent source, and the two App Store versions above do not match.
