# 17 · What he did at X (adviser, head of product, adviser again)

A dated record of his work at X, from his own posts, his Out of Office interview and reporting.
Results are **his claims** unless a third party is cited. Then: what founders can take from it.

## Before the job: adviser to xAI

- Investors at xAI asked him to advise "on product and growth" because of years of posting product
  advice on X [OOO 10:51]. xAI was "about 25 people mostly just AI engineers" when he started; "I was
  advising them for uh a year" [OOO 11:26, 12:05].
- Also a venture partner at Lightspeed and an adviser to the Solana Foundation [TC5].
- The hiring "interview" was a weekend: "we work basically for like 48 hours straight build a
  beautiful prototype of the onboarding flow" for the X app [OOO 14:24].

## Head of product (1 July 2025 to 5 August 2026)

Announced 1 July 2025: "I've officially posted my way to the top." [TC5]

### 1. Restarting growth

- The goal: "for these like mature latestage social networks, they're kind of on a... trajectory uh
  toward irrelevance... I wanted to see uh the next generation adopt it." [OOO 9:06]
- The method: "we audited every every possible funnel to get into the app. Uh, and we threw every growth
  hack in the world at it." [OOO 9:33]
- Hired a growth engineer "on analyzing the effectiveness of product experiments and monitoring the
  health of the X userbase" [X 2025-07-06](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1941655319198958039).
- ⚠️ **App Store results, two versions:** "within I think the first 30 days, we jumped from number 78 in
  the app store to a peak position of number two. We had, uh, double downloads." [OOO 9:33] vs "When I
  joined X two months ago, the app was sitting at No. 78... today we opened at No. 6"
  [X 2025-09-09](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1965421715732759000). Japan: "a 4 year high on the App
  Store: clocking in at No. 11" [X 2025-10-16](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1978930409296601166).
  In June 2026 he repeated the first version: "within the first month we had uh went from 78 in the US
  App Store all the way to a peak position of number two" [TBS 17:35]. (In that interview the host, not
  Bier, cited downloads up 60% since July 2025 and subscriptions over $1B [TBS 18:34].)
  At exit: "We've climbed 70 spots in the App Store since this time last year."
  [X 2026-08-05](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2085105586966827343)

### 2. Getting new users to their niche (onboarding)

- The problem, from his mentor Josh Elman: getting new accounts ramped up "has been one of the app's
  toughest challenges -- and the most important thing to unlock growth"
  [X 2026-01-20](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2013410692444102793).
- Crowdsourced the best accounts per niche [X 2025-11-19](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1991016787543035907),
  then launched **Starterpacks**: "we scoured the world for the top posters in every niche & country...
  to help new users find the best accounts—big or small—for their interests"
  [X 2026-01-21](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2014079005025247240).
- Result he claims: "we've doubled time spent uh in the app for new users", with the biggest gains in
  non-English markets [OOO 27:30].
- **Custom Timelines**: pin one of "over 75 topics" to the home tab, powered by Grok (seen quoted in a
  2026-04-25 reply; launch date not independently confirmed)
  [X 2026-04-25](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2047909972990927255). He calls Custom Timelines and
  Starterpacks "escape valves" for gaps in the For You algorithm.

- How he chooses features: "look at your users and see how are they hacking the platform... that's
  identifying uh, latent demand" [TBS 8:15]. His example is Cashtags, "a runaway success" [TBS 9:32].
- Reply controls: "you can control who can reply to your posts... We call it my network... That has been
  a really resonant feature with women." [TBS 13:07]
- Auto-translate: "we had this huge amount of compute to process every single post on X and translate
  it" [TBS 10:18].
- "The core challenge even since the Twitter days has been activation... we will have succeeded if when a
  plumber signs up for X, they can find other plumbers." [TBS 31:49 to 32:12]

### 3. Fixing why links got low reach

- Diagnosis: "the web browser covers the post and people forget to Like or Reply. So X doesn't get a
  clear signal whether the content is any good." The fix: "posts will now collapse to the bottom of the
  page so people can react while you're reading."
  [X 2025-10-19](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1979994223224209709)
- "It wasn't any intentional act of like demoting links. It was just a byproduct of the UI." [OOO 40:16]
- Result he claims: link impressions "go way up" while time spent stayed "flat" [OOO 38:05]; he says
  "the exact same UI has been adopted by threads and Reddit" [OOO 38:58]. He shipped some of the code
  himself with Claude Code [OOO 40:28].
- He'd shown the link viewer to journalist Alex Heath months earlier to win journalists back [SRC].

### 4. Authenticity: country of origin

- Tested first on X staff profiles [X 2025-10-14](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1978132382868988310),
  then rolled out globally as **About This Account**, with "privacy toggles to only show your region"
  for countries "where speech has penalties"
  [X 2025-11-22](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1992335925322613127).
- Result he claims: "used over 360 million times since launch -- and has one of the highest DAU of any
  new feature we've launched this year (only behind the new link experience)"
  [X 2025-12-02](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1995990576391749850).
- How he built it: users suggested it, it was previewed for feedback, and the region toggle came from
  that feedback [OOO 31:12 to 32:03].

### 7. Chat, money and the rebuild

- XChat: joinable group-chat links (350 members), X Communities deprecated "due to declining usage"
  [X 2026-04-22](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2047041338106159484); standalone XChat app for iOS
  [X 2026-04-24](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2047747631624183889).
- MediaPost: he led rebuilds of "the timeline and Android app", introduced "a creator video-editing hub
  and two standalone apps -- X Money and X Chat" [MP].
- "In the last 12 months, we have rewritten almost every core part of the app. We will soon be shipping
  a 90% reduction in our web app's load times." [X 2026-06-07](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2063767110736908757)
- The timeline algorithm was "rebuilt it from scratch. It was a very tough two months." [TBS 19:48] "We
  rebuilt the notifications tab. We rebuilt the DMs feature as well as ad manager." [TBS 20:15]
- An algorithm migration was judged on one guardrail: "could engagement stay at least flat after the
  the migration. Uh and it did." [OOO 20:42]

### How the team worked

- "There's literally like 30 core product engineers" [OOO 7:57]; "about 30 core engineers on the product.
  Um and uh that has enabled us to move really fast" [TBS 0:58]. "We come up with an idea. Uh we we
  build it in a week and it's it's out, you know, at Facebook things would take months" [OOO 41:15].
- Build in public: preview ideas, gather edge cases, then launch "with like absolute precision"
  [OOO 17:23]. Very little was rolled back "during the last 6 months" [OOO 16:27].

## What founders can take from it

1. **Audit every funnel, then throw every hack at it.** The same method worked on a 20-year-old app as
   on new ones. (ch. 08)
2. **The new-user ramp is the growth lever.** On an interest graph, curated starter content does what
   contact sync does on a friend graph. (ch. 06)
3. **When reach drops, look for a missing signal, not a conspiracy.** Links weren't demoted; the UI hid
   the engagement buttons. (ch. 08)
4. **Ship with a guardrail metric.** Link reach up, time spent flat; algorithm migration flat, then up.
5. **Incentives shape content.** Paying for posts produced bot spam; removing the incentive removed the
   spam. Paying the top accounts less increased DAU in his experiment.
6. **Find features where users are already hacking the product** (latent demand, ch. 01).
7. **Preview in public, then launch precisely.** Country of origin shipped with a privacy toggle because
   the preview surfaced the risk. (ch. 13)
