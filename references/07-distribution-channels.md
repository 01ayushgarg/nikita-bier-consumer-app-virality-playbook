# 07 · Distribution: how apps actually grow

## Only two product channels

> "The only way apps can grow aside from word of mouth is uh referrals like invitations like onetoone
> uh acquisition and then the other is uh sharing one to many. So like you know posting on your story
> uh and uh you really have to unpack those funnels." [OOO 1:05:46]

## A viral TikTok is a press bump, not product-led growth

> "A lot of people conflate, you know, going viral on Tik Tok, having a a video about your product,
> but that's that's not... It's kind of like a press bump. True productled growth is where uh the
> mechanics of the product uh uh like are actually driving the acquisition." [OOO 1:06:05 to 1:06:14]

## K-factor is found in increments

> "To get virality uh you you have to just incrementally find bits of K factor, which is uh because you
> need to get to a 1.0 K factor to be truly viral." [OOO 1:04:58]

For utility apps that will never reach 1.0, he still pushes K-factor up and then adds paid
acquisition (captions unclear on the exact wording) [OOO 1:05:16 to 1:05:38].

## Marketing and product are one funnel

> "A lot of founders separate marketing and product growth, like top of funnel growth from the
> actual products growth mechanisms, but they're both the same." [LP 1:33:07]

If you target a community and want saturation [LP 1:33:24 to 1:33:57]:
1. **The ad** shows imagery of that community.
2. **Inside the app**, the user can join that community.
3. **The invite** mentions that community.

> "You need to actually cover everything from the ads to the in-app experience. All of that
> needs to be aligned for a user acquisition and flywheel to spin. A lot of people really screw
> that up."

## Rename when the name can't sell the download

> "Sometimes I meet with a I met with a company and I'm like we need to rename the app... Because it
> was hard to communicate the value proposition... the conversion rates to download were really
> poor." [OOO 1:06:32 to 1:06:40]

His example: a longevity app he advised was renamed to say "exactly what the product did", which
also made it a press story (the auto-captions render the new name as "dev clock") [OOO 1:06:48].

## Use a platform's share surface, but expect it to be taken away

In October 2022 Nikita said 23% of Snapchat's US user base had viewed a Gas story; sharing to
Snapchat was a primary button in the app [TC4]. Then:

> "Two years ago, I met with Snapchat's CEO to discuss acquiring my previous company. I openly shared
> how fast we were growing. Just a week later — over the Thanksgiving holiday — Snapchat kicked our app
> off the SnapKit platform, abruptly halting our growth." [TC4]

**Lesson:** a one-to-many channel you don't own can disappear overnight. Keep a channel you own.

**Checks to run:**
- Which of the two channels (one-to-one invites, one-to-many shares) is instrumented end to end?
- Is a spike from a viral video being mistaken for product-led growth?
- Does the community in the ad match the one in the app and in the invite?
- Does the app's name say what it does? What is the store-page-to-download conversion?
