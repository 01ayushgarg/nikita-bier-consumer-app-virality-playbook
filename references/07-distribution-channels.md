> onetoone uh acquisition and then the other is uh sharing one to many." [OOO 1:05:50]

One-to-one invites are covered in chapter 06. One-to-many sharing, and why a one-off share feature

## A viral TikTok is a press bump, not product-led growth

> "it's kind of like a press bump. true productled growth is where uh the mechanics of the product uh
> uh like are actually driving the acquisition." [OOO 1:06:14]

## K-factor is found in increments

> "you have to just incrementally find bits of k factor, which is uh because you need to get to a 1.0
> k factor to be truly viral." [OOO 1:05:11]

For utility apps there may be no path to “a full 1.0k factor”, so he aims to raise it and then
"augment that with paid acquisition" (the captions make the speaker of that last phrase unclear)
[OOO 1:05:28].

## Marketing and product are one funnel

> "a lot of founders separate marketing and product growth, like top of funnel growth from the actual
> products growth mechanisms, but they're both the same." [LP 1:33:09]

If you target a community [LP 1:33:31 to 1:33:52]:
1. **The ad** shows imagery of that community.
2. **Inside the app**, the user can join that community.
3. **The invite** mentions that community.

> "you need to actually cover everything from the ads to the in-app experience. all of that needs to
> be aligned for a user acquisition and flywheel to spin." [LP 1:33:52]

## Rename when the name can't sell the download

He told one company to rename "because it was hard to communicate the value proposition... the
conversion rates to download were really poor." [OOO 1:06:36 to 1:06:40] His example was a longevity
app renamed to say exactly what it did (the captions render the name as “dev clock”) [OOO 1:06:57].

## Use a platform's share surface, but expect it to be taken away

In October 2022 Nikita said 23% of Snapchat's US users had viewed a Gas story, and sharing to
Snapchat was a main button in the app [TC4]. Then, as he told TechCrunch, a week after he met
Snapchat's CEO to discuss an acquisition, "Snapchat kicked our app off the SnapKit platform, abruptly
halting our growth" [TC4].

## How to apply it (our reading)

1. **Attribute every install** to one of: invite, share, organic search, paid, press or creator
   content, unknown (`templates/01-instrumentation-checklist.md`).
2. **Split product-led from bought.** Invites and shares are product channels; creator videos and
   ads are bought or borrowed attention.
3. **Write the community line** for the ad, the first screen and the invite text. If the three
   don't name the same group, fix that first.
4. **Check owned vs rented.** For each channel, ask who could switch it off.

**Worked numbers (invented).** 10,000 installs a month: 55% creator videos, 25% search, 15% invites,
5% shares. Product-led share is 20%. If the creator budget stopped tomorrow, more than half of
growth would go with it. Our suggestion: treat creator content as a test of the message, and judge
the product by whether the 20% grows week on week.

**Failure modes.**
- Reading a spike after a viral video as product-market fit.
- Ads aimed at one community, invites written for everyone.
- One rented channel (a platform share kit) carrying most of the growth.

**Limits.** The install figures for the #1 rank are his 2024 estimates and change with ad spend.
