# 07 · Marketing and product are one funnel

> "A lot of founders separate marketing and product growth, like top of funnel growth from the
> actual products growth mechanisms, but they're both the same." (1:33:07)

If you target a community and want saturation (1:33:24 to 1:33:57):
1. **The ad** shows imagery of that community.
2. **Inside the app**, the user can join that community.
3. **The invite** mentions that community.

> "You need to actually cover everything from the ads to the in-app experience. All of that
> needs to be aligned for a user acquisition and flywheel to spin. A lot of people really screw
> that up."

**Checks to run:**
- Write down the community named in the ad, the first screen, and the invite text. Are they the same?
- Does a new user land in a group of people like them, or in a generic empty state?
- Do invites carry the community's name, so the receiver knows why they are invited?
