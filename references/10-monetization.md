# 10 · Making it pay

## Charge for the most-requested thing

On tbh, "the number one support message we received was can I pay to reveal who sent me polls?"
Nikita kept asking himself whether monetizing would have made more than the acquisition (56:23 to 56:40).
Gas was monetized. Lenny cites $11 million in sales and 10 million downloads, and
Nikita confirms (1:09:58).

**Checks to run:**
- What is the top request in support? Is it something users would pay for?
- Is the paid feature the answer to curiosity or desire the free product creates?
- Validate payment only after the earlier rungs hold (see `04-validation-ladder.md`).

## Lean teams

tbh was two engineers, one designer and Nikita when Facebook's team visited, in an office that
cost $1,800 a month (37:50 to 38:11). Nikita on proceeds: after an IPO and "seven rounds of
dilution," many founders end up with amounts "pretty comparable to what we get from our apps
for 90 days of work." (1:22:21)

This is context, not a rule. Whether to raise venture capital is the user's decision.
