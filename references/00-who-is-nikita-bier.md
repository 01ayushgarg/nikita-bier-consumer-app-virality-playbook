## The track record behind the advice

- Two #1 apps in the US App Store and two acquisitions, each with a tiny team. When Facebook visited
  the tbh office: "There's two engineers and one designer and me". [LP 38:02], [TC2]
- tbh: "5 million downloads and 2.5 million daily active users in the past nine weeks". [TC2]
- Gas: see the revenue and install conflicts in `SOURCES.md`. Every figure points to millions of
  users and several million dollars of revenue within months.
- At X: he says the app went from number 78 to a peak of number two in the US App Store, and he
  hedges the time window. [OOO 9:33]. Details in chapter 17.

## Read him with his own caveats

> "Retention for consumer social is there's a tremendous amount of randomness." [LP 1:18:51]

> "Many of these kind of viral apps have a short shelf life... a lot of these viral apps basically are
> like uh films, you know, you you watch it, it has this cultural impact and then uh and then people
> move on" [OOO 54:31]

He says about half of the companies he advises hit big success and half fail outright,
"because consumer is so random." [LP 1:29:37]

## Why the 15th app worked, in his words

> "we built 15 apps um we were about to shut down we were very close to it and the 15th app went
> straight to number one in the app store... it was our hail mary but at the same time it was sort of
> a magnum opus of everything we had learned over the years" [WIH 7:02]

> "when it hit we knew exactly what to do we knew exactly how to scale it" [WIH 8:26]

His advice to students in 2018: "Just keep going. No one said it was going to be easy... No promises
though." [SCET 1:41] A write-up of the same evening reports him telling students to "Expect the
first idea to be wrong, but it might lead you to the right idea." [SCETW]

## How to use this chapter (our reading)

The track record matters for one reason: it tells you which advice comes from repeated tests (the
testing method, onboarding, invites, density) and which comes from one or two events (selling a
company, a hoax, running a large platform). Weigh the first kind more heavily. Where he gives a
number, `benchmarks.md` lists it with its date and source.
