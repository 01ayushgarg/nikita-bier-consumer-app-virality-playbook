# 06 · Invites, naming and contacts

## The name decides the invite (1:15:52 to 1:16:59)

- Gas went through names including Crush and Melt. Crush had a great domain, but under that
  name "invitations dropped significantly."
- The cause: "Boys invite boys, girls invite girls to apps," and "boys didn't want to invite
  their friends to an app called Crush with a pink icon."
- tbh and Gas indexed about 60 to 65% women. They made the app "more masculine": black icon,
  a flame, named Gas. "The invites rate jumped."
- "You think a name doesn't matter, but right at the moment of sending an invite..."

**Check:** read the name and icon from the point of view of the least likely inviter in the
audience. Would they send it to a friend? Measure invite rate across name tests, not just installs.

## Invites the user sends knowingly

- On tbh, you tapped a contact's name, tapped Invite, and the app sent a text via Twilio (58:19).
- Many clones assumed tbh texted people when they were voted on: "We never did that. That's
  egregiously illegal to do and also unethical at a user experience level." (58:43)
- Later rules meant texts had to be sent from the user's own device, so users had to open the
  compose window and press send for each invite, and fewer invites went out (59:00).

## Contact permissions on iOS 18 `[2024]`

Present this section as of mid-2024 and tell the user to re-check the current iOS behaviour.

- About 65% of users approve the contacts permission across apps, higher for teens, lower for adults (1:24:27).
- iOS 18 added a step where the user picks which contacts to share from an alphabetical list.
  His expectation: "it's going to be very difficult to find friends on apps going forward" (1:25:25).
- "If you're betting on contact sync as a company right now, you better start thinking about plan B." (1:26:06)
- His view: most apps will not have social graphs going forward, which "will entrench incumbents even more." (1:26:23)

**Checks to run:**
- What is the invite rate per active user, and invites per inviter? Track them weekly.
- Is every invite clearly sent by the user, from a screen they understand?
- If the plan depends on contact sync: what is plan B?
