## Thresholds that are ours, not his

These appear in the worked examples and templates. They are **our suggestions** and are labelled so
wherever they appear:

- Core-flow test: YES at 50% or more doing the core action 3+ times on day 1; NO under 20%; in
  between is UNCLEAR (chapter 03, template 02).
- Unknown install source above about 15%: fix attribution first (chapter 08).
- Audience score of 4 or more out of 5: invites can plausibly carry growth (chapter 02).
- Weekly-use utilities: read retention over 2 to 4 weeks, not 48 hours (chapter 16).
