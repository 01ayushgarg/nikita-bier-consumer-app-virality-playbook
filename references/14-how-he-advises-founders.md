# 14 · How he advises founders

Use this file to run an audit the way he describes running his.

## What he offers

- Intro listing, as shown on 3 Oct 2026: "Former CPO of X. Founder of Gas (acquired by Discord).
  Founder of TBH (acquired by Meta)." A 15-minute session at $7,500, and "Sessions will only be
  accepted for venture-backed companies." [INTRO]
- In July 2024 he described a $10k-a-month advisory. Paraphrased, he promised: knowing how onboarding
  and share funnels will convert before they are built, iOS API tricks, access to designers and
  architects, and repositioning the app around its core value.
  [X 2024-07-21](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1815130311963168831) In the same post he says four
  apps reached the Top 10 after subscribing and one went from $0 to "$2.5m ARR in 90 days".
  **His claim, unverified.**

## His engagement, step by step

0. **Vision and audience first.** He asks for the vision first, then the
   target audience [SOL 0:37].
1. **Show me the analytics.** How people distribute the app today, the activation milestone, and
   what's in the way [LP 1:32:44].
2. **How is it growing today?** Find existing organic distribution and where downloads come from
   [OOO 1:08:14].
3. **Look at every funnel**, from ad to in-app to invite, as one system [LP 1:33:09].
4. **Table stakes, then 2 to 3 step-function changes.** "get all the table stakes, grow things out of
   the way, at the minimum. then identify two to three step function changes that could change their
   growth trajectory." [LP 1:35:04]
5. **Get into the pixels.** "i go into figma and we do a live session together and clean things up."
   He calls out what each screen is “going to convert at” [LP 1:35:29].
6. **Leave no stone unturned**: no silver bullet [OOO 1:04:50].

His target for clients: "i try to make them 10 times back their money in the first 30 days." [LP 1:34:46]

## How to run a session like this (our reading)

**Before the call (founder sends):** analytics access or a funnel export, install sources, a screen
recording of the first 60 seconds, the vision in one line, the target audience.

**During (60 minutes, our split):**

| Minutes | Do | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | Vision and audience | One line each |
| 5 to 20 | Analytics: sources, activation milestone, drop-offs | The first unproven rung (chapter 04) |
| 20 to 35 | Walk the screens; predict each conversion out loud | Table stakes list |
| 35 to 50 | Two or three step-function ideas | Ranked by expected effect |
| 50 to 60 | Pick one and design its test | A 48-hour plan with thresholds |

**After:** a written audit in the format in `SKILL.md`, every point tied to a chapter.

**Failure modes.**
- Starting with ideas before seeing the data.
- A list of 20 table-stakes fixes and no step-function bet.
- Step-function bets with no test attached.

**Limits.** His results are his own reports. The half-succeed, half-fail ratio is the honest prior
for any engagement.
