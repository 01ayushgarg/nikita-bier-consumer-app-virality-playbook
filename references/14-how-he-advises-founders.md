## His engagement, step by step

1. **Show me the analytics.** "How people are distributing the app today, what is the milestone that
   a user must hit to become activated and what's getting in the way of that?" [LP 1:32:44]
2. **How is it growing today?** Find existing organic distribution and attribute where downloads come
   from, then amplify it [OOO 1:08:12].
3. **Look at every funnel**, from ad to in-app to invite, as one system [LP 1:33:07].
4. **Clear the table stakes, then 2 to 3 step-function changes.** "Get all the table stakes, grow
   things out of the way, at the minimum. Then identify two to three step function changes that could
   change their growth trajectory. And these are higher scope fundamental changes to the product."
   [LP 1:35:03]
5. **Get into the pixels.** "Once they settle on a direction, I tend to get in the pixels. I go into
   Figma and we do a live session together and clean things up. I identify, 'Oh, that's going to
   convert at this percent.'" [LP 1:35:28]
6. **Juice every surface** so happy users can tell friends; no silver bullet [OOO 1:04:49].

His goal for clients: "I try to make them 10 times back their money in the first 30 days." [LP 1:34:49]
