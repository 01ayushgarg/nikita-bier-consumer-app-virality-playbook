1. Read [`../SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md) first and follow its file-reading order.
2. Read [`../references/benchmarks.md`](../references/benchmarks.md) before using any number, metric, or benchmark.
3. Load only the reference chapters and templates that `SKILL.md` names for the user's stage or validation-ladder rung.

5. Cite source IDs from the relevant reference chapters and never attribute uncited claims to Nikita Bier.
6. Label repo-authored thresholds, examples, and recommendations as "our reading" or "our suggestion" when they are not his sourced words.
7. Apply the positive-design guardrail pass from [`../references/12-positive-design-and-guardrails.md`](../references/12-positive-design-and-guardrails.md) to every recommendation.
