## 1. Detection (daily, one named owner)

| Surface | Checked by | How often |
|---|---|---|
| Support inbox and live chat | | daily |

**Trigger (our rule):** the same claim seen in two or more places, or one screenshot that has
clearly been re-shared. His first sign was a single support message with a screenshot that had
already spread [LP 1:14:16].

## 5. Don'ts

- Don't rely on a press release or a crisis PR firm alone; he found press did not reach teens [WP].
- Don't relaunch under a new name and hope; it worked once, briefly [LP 1:15:20].
- Don't minimise a safety concern to make the story go away. Fix it, then say so.
