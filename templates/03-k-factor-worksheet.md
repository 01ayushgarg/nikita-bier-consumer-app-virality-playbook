**Note:** the formula below is the standard industry definition of K-factor, not something Nikita
published. The quotes above are his.

## The formula

```
K = (share of new users who invite or share)
  × (invites or shares sent per inviting user)
  × (share of invites or shares that become a new user)
```

K above 1.0 means each cohort of users brings in a larger one. Below 1.0, growth from invites fades
and must be topped up by other channels.

## For apps that won't reach 1.0

Many utility apps never reach 1.0. Nikita's approach with them was still to push K up, then add paid
acquisition (captions unclear on exact wording) [OOO 1:05:16 to 1:05:38]. Every 0.1 of K makes
each paid install bring in more users for free.
